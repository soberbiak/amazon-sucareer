# Experience-level proof collection

Use years to choose what evidence to request, not what seniority to assign.

## Record separately

- total work years and Amazon-relevant years;
- largest operating unit: link, product set, account, product line, category, market, team, or business unit;
- decisions personally owned;
- metrics personally reviewed and outcomes accountable for;
- planning horizon: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annual;
- mechanisms built and number of cycles repeated;
- budget, inventory, product, supplier, or people resources influenced;
- formal and informal leadership evidence.

## 1–3 year proof

Prioritize one complete closed loop. Collect the starting problem, owned execution, decision adjustments, result, and review. Verify whether “independent operation” meant genuine decision rights or merely executing a manager's plan.

## 3–5 year proof

Prioritize product-line or category decisions across traffic, conversion, advertising, profit, and inventory. Look for repeated outcomes, playbooks, cross-functional influence, and trade-offs. Record team leadership only when hiring, goal allocation, coaching, review, or performance responsibility actually existed.

## 5–10 year proof

Prioritize annual/quarterly plans, goal decomposition, P&L-like accountability, portfolio and budget choices, multi-market complexity, organization design, operating cadence, talent development, and durable results across cycles. For principal specialists without direct reports, collect evidence of broad decision influence and reusable expert systems.

## Gap labels

- `tenure_scope_gap`: years exceed demonstrated operating responsibility;
- `next_level_proof_gap`: some next-level actions exist but outcomes or repeatability are missing;
- `management_evidence_gap`: management title without people-system evidence;
- `business_ownership_gap`: sales claims without profit, inventory, resource, or risk accountability;
- `scale_context_gap`: result lacks product, market, budget, team, or time scale.
