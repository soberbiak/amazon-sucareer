# Scoring rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 4.

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Specificity | no concrete context | partial scope or sequence | precise scope, period, sequence, and decision point |
| Evidence | unsupported assertion | some source or metric detail | traceable evidence with correct comparison and uncertainty |
| Ownership | cannot separate team work | identifies some personal work | clearly separates own decisions, execution, and dependencies |
| Mechanism | no explanation | plausible but shallow | explains causal chain, assumptions, and failure conditions |
| Trade-offs | none considered | names an alternative | compares options, constraints, risks, and stopping rule |
| Reflection | no learning | generic lesson | specific failure, revision, and transferable insight |
| Communication | incoherent or evasive | understandable but loose | concise top line followed by structured supporting detail |

## Interpretation

- `24–28`: strong and defensible; preserve claim.
- `18–23`: usable; repair one or two weak dimensions.
- `11–17`: high follow-up risk; narrow wording and practice.
- `0–10`: remove or substantially rewrite until evidence and knowledge improve.

The total is a coaching signal, not a hiring prediction. A single zero in Evidence or Ownership can still make a claim unsafe even when the total is moderate.

## Recommended answer structure

Use a short headline followed by detail:

```text
Conclusion → scoped context → diagnosis and decision → personal actions → result and evidence → dependencies → learning
```

Do not memorize model prose. Preserve the candidate's own vocabulary and only improve structure after the facts are stable.
