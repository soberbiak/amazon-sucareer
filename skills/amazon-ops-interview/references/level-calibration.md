# Interview depth by experience band

Use the target JD's responsibility and the candidate's actual scope together. A hard question should test the next expected layer, not demand fictional experience.

## 1–3 years

Test whether the candidate genuinely operated a complete loop:

- exact account/product scope and daily decisions;
- Listing, advertising, inventory, account-health, and promotion mechanics;
- baseline, denominator, action sequence, and result;
- what the candidate changed after data feedback;
- where manager instructions ended and personal judgment began.

Red flag: tool vocabulary without a complete case.

## 3–5 years — senior IC

Test product-line or category judgment:

- resource allocation across products and lifecycle stages;
- sales, profit, advertising, and inventory trade-offs;
- counterfactuals and alternative strategies;
- repeated mechanisms rather than one lucky result;
- influence on product, creative, supply, or finance partners.

Red flag: several years of isolated execution with no scope growth or reusable method.

## 3–5 years — lead or manager

Add questions about target allocation, review cadence, coaching, delegation, underperformance, and the difference between personal output and team throughput.

Red flag: “带团队” means distributing tasks but not setting goals, improving capability, or owning team results.

## 5–10 years — manager or business owner

Test annual and quarterly planning, P&L logic, portfolio choices, budget and headcount allocation, multi-market risk, operating mechanisms, cross-functional conflict, hiring/coaching, and sustained outcomes across business cycles.

Red flag: a manager title supported only by hands-on store operations.

## 5–10 years — principal specialist

Do not force people-management questions. Test whether expertise changes high-value decisions across products, markets, or teams; whether frameworks are reused by others; and whether influence produces durable business outcomes.

Red flag: deep tool execution without broader decision influence.

## Calibration output

For every important mismatch, label one of:

- `level_aligned`;
- `stretch_but_defensible`;
- `years_without_scope`;
- `title_without_management`;
- `strong_ic_misrouted_to_manager`;
- `manager_claim_without_business_ownership`.
