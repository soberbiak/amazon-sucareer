# Resume QA

## Evidence

- Every important bullet maps to one or more claim IDs.
- Unsupported or `private_only` claims are absent.
- Percentage points, percentages, currencies, periods, and scope are correct.
- Team outcomes retain the candidate's actual responsibility level.

## Positioning

- The target level is plausible from the evidence.
- The market band and actual archetype were calibrated separately.
- A 3–5 year candidate was not forced into management, and a 5–10 year candidate was not upgraded on tenure alone.
- Conservative and stretch positioning differ by evidence depth, not invented scope.
- The first half page contains the highest-value proof.
- Summary, skills, experience, and projects do not repeat the same sentence.
- Campus projects are labeled; experienced work shows ownership and mechanisms.

## Writing

- The resume follows [the Resume Expression Contract](resume-expression-contract.md): concise candidate-facing bullets, complete supporting evidence.
- One main claim per bullet.
- Short topic labels improve scanning only when they add information and do not repeat the sentence.
- Company and project summaries state operating scope and main responsibility; evidence caveats do not replace the role summary.
- Bullets follow the priority order of operating mechanism, clearest result or output, then sustaining collaboration or review method.
- A bullet has no more than two bold anchors; each is an evidence-supported topic, result, scope, count, or deliverable.
- Work, internship, and project bullets, as well as personal summary lines, omit terminal `。` and `；` by default.
- Personal summary is one to two concise lines stating core scope and one differentiating strength; it does not repeat bullet or skills content.
- Concrete nouns and verbs replace unsupported adjectives.
- Abbreviations are conventional or explained once.
- Rates use percentage points for absolute changes;同比、环比 and intervention comparisons are labeled by their true baseline.
- Comparison details and guardrail metrics appear only when they materially improve interpretation.
- Mixed Chinese/English typography is consistent.
- No keyword stuffing, fake proficiency bars, self-ratings, or generic personality claims.

## Layout

- One page unless evidence genuinely requires two.
- No portrait by default and no empty placeholder.
- Body text remains readable at print size.
- Dates and locations align consistently.
- No clipped content, orphan headings, or large unused gaps.
- Links work in the exported PDF when included.
