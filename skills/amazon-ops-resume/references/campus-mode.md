# Campus mode

Campus resumes sell credible potential, not fictional seniority.

## Recommended order

1. name, target role, contact/location;
2. concise positioning summary;
3. education and relevant coursework only when useful;
4. internships and practical projects;
5. selected skills, languages, and certifications;
6. optional campus leadership or awards with job-relevant evidence.

## Project evidence pattern

Use this sequence without forcing every part into one sentence:

```text
Context and objective → problem decomposition → personal execution → observable output → review and learning
```

Acceptable evidence can include a labeled simulation, public dataset analysis, internship task, marketplace research, listing audit, advertising analysis, inventory exercise, or bilingual content project. State what was real and what was simulated.

## Strong campus signals

- asks a testable question instead of saying “熟悉运营”；
- explains dataset, sample, time window, and method;
- produces an artifact such as a keyword map, listing audit, forecast, experiment plan, or dashboard;
- distinguishes recommendation from actual business result;
- reflects on error, uncertainty, or next validation step.

## Forbidden upgrades

- “参与模拟店铺” → “负责店铺运营”；
- “提出建议” → “推动销售增长”；
- “学习过广告结构” → “精通广告投放”；
- class data → real company performance;
- team award → individual commercial outcome.

When no business result exists, report the quality of analysis or deliverable rather than inventing revenue impact.
