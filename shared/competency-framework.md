# Amazon operations competency framework

Use this framework to interpret evidence, not to manufacture missing capability. Tag depth as `core`, `supporting`, `exposure`, or `no_evidence`.

| Competency | Representative mechanisms | Typical business value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketplace operations | account cadence, issue resolution, operational control | continuity and execution quality |
| Advertising operations | search-intent matching, bid efficiency, budget allocation | traffic efficiency and profitable demand capture |
| Listing and conversion | discoverability, information quality, conversion diagnostics | lower purchase friction and stronger conversion |
| Pricing and promotion | price architecture, promotion economics, demand response | volume-margin balance |
| Inventory and supply chain | forecasting, coverage, lead-time planning, inbound control | availability and working-capital efficiency |
| Product and market analysis | demand signals, competition, assortment choices | better portfolio and launch decisions |
| Financial awareness | revenue decomposition, margin bridge, cost structure | profitable growth and resource allocation |
| Data and analytics | metric design, variance analysis, diagnostics | decision quality and faster detection |
| Risk and compliance | account health, policy controls, escalation | business continuity and loss prevention |
| Project management | milestones, dependencies, risk control | reliable cross-functional delivery |
| Cross-functional collaboration | decision alignment, handoffs, trade-offs | coordinated execution |
| Leadership | direction, delegation, coaching, accountability | team or function performance |
| Automation and AI | workflow design, quality controls, human review | repeatability and operating leverage |

Breadth is not a label count. Repeated owned decisions across contexts support core capability; occasional participation supports exposure only. Tool use alone does not prove analytical, strategic, or leadership capability.
