# Attribution rules

## Responsibility

- `owner`: set the approach and was accountable for the scoped decision or outcome.
- `co_owner`: shared material decisions and accountability with other roles.
- `contributor`: executed or analyzed a meaningful part without owning the whole outcome.
- `observer`: learned from or supported the work without material decision authority.

Prefer the narrower defensible level. Record the business outcome, the candidate-owned action, and the link between them as separate facts.

## Wording ladder

Use the strongest supported wording only:

1. `delivered`: controlled intervention, stable scope, clear comparison, and limited alternative explanations.
2. `enabled`: substantial ownership with dependencies named.
3. `contributed`: shared outcome or incomplete isolation of the candidate's effect.
4. `observed`: timing relationship only.

Large account or team results do not become individual results because the candidate worked on the account. If the link is weak, state the candidate's owned action and describe the result as a team or business result.
